# Tearoff — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 25, 2026_

Tearoff is a receipt vault that tracks the return and warranty deadlines on
things you buy. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with
your data. It is deliberately short because Tearoff collects very little.

## The short version

- Your receipts and purchases stay **on your device**, and — if you are signed
  into iCloud — in **your own private iCloud account** so they sync across your
  devices. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
- Tearoff has **no accounts, no login, no servers of our own, and no ads.**
- We do **not** collect analytics, track you, or sell or share any data.

## What Tearoff stores, and where

Everything you enter or scan — merchant names, amounts, dates, categories,
notes, barcodes, and receipt images — is stored locally on your device using
Apple's on-device database.

If you are signed into iCloud, this data also syncs through **CloudKit** to your
personal, private iCloud account. That sync is between your own devices under
your Apple ID; the developer of Tearoff cannot see it. Apple's handling of
iCloud data is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

## Camera

On the Pro tier, Tearoff can use your device camera to scan paper receipts. The
camera is used **only** while you are actively scanning. Scanned images are
processed on your device (including text recognition) and stored with the
matching purchase. Images are not uploaded to us.

## Location

On the Pro tier, Tearoff can optionally use your location to tag a purchase
with its store and to notify you if you're nearby later with the return
window still open. This is opt-in (off by default), checked only while the
app is open in the foreground, and never runs in the background. Your
location is never stored beyond the one coordinate you choose to save with a
purchase, and it is never transmitted anywhere.

## Diagnostics

Tearoff keeps a small, **counts-only** accuracy ledger on your device to gauge
how well receipt extraction is working (for example, how often a value was read
correctly). This ledger stores **no receipt contents** and **never leaves your
device**. It is not transmitted anywhere.

## Purchases

Pro is sold as an in-app purchase through Apple. Payment is handled entirely by
Apple; Tearoff never sees your payment details. Purchase validation uses Apple's
StoreKit on your device.

## What we do not do

- We do not run any server that receives your data.
- We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.

## Data deletion

Because your data lives on your device (and your private iCloud), you are in
control of it. Deleting a purchase removes it; deleting the app removes its
local data. iCloud copies are managed through your Apple ID settings.

## Children

Tearoff is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal
information from anyone, of any age — it collects no personal information at all.

## Changes
